Privacy Policy ♡

This policy explains how Amora handles information when you use its social features, stories, anonymous message links and private conversations.

Effective date: 25 October 2026

Important: Anonymous means your account identity is not displayed to the message recipient as part of the anonymous-message experience. It is not a promise that no technical records exist or that a person can never be identified.

1. Information we handle

The information involved depends on which Amora features you use. It may include:

  • Account information: email address used for sign-in, username, display name, profile details and account identifiers.
  • Content you create: posts, photos or videos you upload, comments, replies, likes, reposts, follows, profile traits and stories.
  • Messages: anonymous messages sent through a link, private chat messages, guest conversations and information needed to deliver those messages.
  • Safety information: reports, moderation decisions and limited records used to detect spam, abuse or attempts to misuse the service, where those features are enabled.
  • Technical information: information processed by the website, hosting, authentication or database services to operate, secure and troubleshoot Amora. The exact technical logs retained can depend on those providers.
  • Device permissions: photos, camera or video are accessed when you choose to use a feature that requests them. Do not upload content you do not have permission to share.

Do not include passwords, recovery tokens, financial details or other highly sensitive information in posts or messages. Amora does not need those details to provide ordinary social features.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to create and secure accounts; display profiles and content to the audiences selected; deliver posts, stories, comments, messages and notifications; support guest-chat recovery when you explicitly choose to link it; enforce content rules; investigate reports and abuse; prevent spam; maintain and troubleshoot the service; and comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not promise that every feature is available at all times. Features and their controls may change as Amora develops.

3. What other people can see

  • Public content: public posts, public profile information and interactions may be visible to other users and may be copied or shared by people who can view them.
  • Audience-limited content: content marked for a restricted audience is intended to be shown according to the audience controls implemented for that feature. Check the audience before posting.
  • Stories: stories are designed to expire from normal viewing after 24 hours. Expiry does not necessarily remove copies someone made, reports, moderation records or backups immediately.
  • Private conversations: messages are intended for the participants in a conversation. Do not assume this means end-to-end encryption; this policy does not claim that Amora provides it.
  • Account details: your sign-in email and internal account identifiers are not intended to be displayed as ordinary public profile information.

Anything another person has already seen, captured, copied or shared may remain outside your control even if you later delete the original content.

4. Anonymous messages and guest conversations

When someone sends an anonymous message through an Amora link, the recipient-facing experience is designed not to display the sender's account identity. Amora's systems may still process technical information, message records, identifiers or security records needed to deliver messages, support conversations, prevent abuse or enforce the rules.

Do not treat anonymity as absolute untraceability. The content of a message, details voluntarily included by the sender, device or network records held by service providers, or a lawful request may reveal information beyond the recipient-facing display.

Guest conversations and recovery features may use tokens or technical identifiers to associate a guest with their conversation. Keep recovery tokens private. If you choose to link guest history to an account, that history may become associated with the account according to the linking flow and its consent step.

We do not promise that a recipient can reply directly to every anonymous message. Replying is available only through the supported conversation flow shown by Amora.

5. Service providers and disclosures

Amora uses third-party infrastructure to operate the website and its data services, including Supabase for authentication and database functionality and Vercel for website hosting and delivery. These providers may process information as needed to provide, secure and maintain their services under their own terms and privacy documentation.

Information may also be disclosed where necessary to protect users or the service, investigate suspected abuse, enforce applicable rules, respond to valid legal obligations, or address a security incident. We do not sell your personal information to advertisers as a stated purpose of this policy.

6. Storage, retention and deletion

Information is retained for as long as needed to operate the relevant feature, maintain account and conversation history, address safety or security issues, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. The exact retention period may vary by information type and technical backup or log systems.

Deleting a post, message or account may not immediately erase every related copy from backups, technical logs, reports or records that must be retained for legitimate safety or legal reasons. We should not be understood to guarantee a particular deletion timetable unless Amora explicitly provides one for that feature.

Stories are intended to stop appearing in normal story viewing after 24 hours. This is an expiry of normal availability, not a guarantee of immediate erasure from all storage or copies.

7. Your choices and privacy rights

Use the controls available in Amora to review or change profile details, choose a post audience, remove content where supported, and manage your appearance preferences. You can choose what to include in a post or message, and should avoid publishing information you want to keep private.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of or restriction of certain personal information, or to object to certain processing. Those rights can have legal exceptions. To make a privacy request, use Amora's official contact channel when one is provided and identify the account concerned without sending your password or recovery token.

If a dedicated privacy contact is not displayed in the app, that is a contact detail Amora still needs to publish. This page does not invent an email address or claim that an unimplemented account-deletion control exists.

8. Security and safety

Amora uses database access controls and application logic intended to restrict access to private information. No internet service can guarantee perfect security. Keep your sign-in credentials and recovery tokens confidential, use a secure device, and report suspected unauthorized access or abuse through the official channel available to you.

Access by authorized operators or service providers may be necessary for maintenance, security, moderation or legal compliance. Do not assume that private messages are technically inaccessible to all service operators.

9. Children and age

Use Amora only if you meet the minimum age and eligibility requirements that apply to you and your location. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact Amora through its official contact channel when available so the situation can be reviewed.

10. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when Amora's features, data practices or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top indicates when this version was prepared. Check this page periodically for updates.

For privacy questions or requests, use the official Amora contact method published on the website or in the app. A dedicated privacy email or formal request channel should be added here before launch if one is established.

This policy is a plain-language explanation, not legal advice or a guarantee that every listed practice has been independently audited. Amora's operator should review it against the live implementation and applicable data-protection law before treating it as final legal compliance documentation.
